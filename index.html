Pet Party（萌寵派對）隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日（加入 Firebase 說明）

開發者：Pet Party（聯絡方式見文末）

遊戲資料存在你的手機裡

遊戲進度、金幣、設定都只存在你的手機裡。我們沒有自己的伺服器，不會要求你註冊或登入，也不會讀取你的聯絡人、照片、精確位置。

數據分析與當機回報（Google Firebase）

為了改善遊戲，我們使用 Google Firebase 的 Analytics（數據分析）、Crashlytics（當機回報）和 Remote Config（遠端設定）。它們會收集：App 執行個體 ID、裝置型號與系統版本、遊戲內的使用事件（例如開了哪款遊戲、過了第幾關、看了廣告）、當機紀錄。這些資料不包含你的姓名或聯絡方式，只用來了解大家怎麼玩、找出錯誤。 在歐洲經濟區、英國等地區，你拒絕個人化同意時，不會儲存廣告相關的資料。 Firebase 的隱私說明：https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

廣告（Google AdMob）

遊戲用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能會使用廣告 ID、裝置資訊、大概的位置（由 IP 推估）來顯示和評估廣告。 在歐洲經濟區、英國等地區，第一次開啟時會詢問你是否同意個人化廣告，之後可以在遊戲「設定 → 隱私選項（廣告）」隨時更改。 Google 如何使用資料：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

購買（Google Play 結帳）

「去除廣告」透過 Google Play 付款。付款資料由 Google 處理，我們看不到你的信用卡或付款資訊。

備份

Android 的自動備份可能會把遊戲進度存到你自己的 Google 帳號，方便換手機時還原。這由 Google 處理，你可以在手機設定裡關閉。

兒童

本遊戲的目標對象是 13 歲以上的玩家，不會刻意收集兒童的資料。

刪除資料

解除安裝遊戲就會刪除手機上的所有遊戲資料。

Pet Party Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026 (added Firebase)

Developer: Pet Party (see contact below)

Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers of our own, no account or login, and we do not access your contacts, photos or precise location.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To improve the game we use Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config. They collect: an app instance ID, device model and OS version, in-game usage events (for example which game you opened, which level you cleared, ads watched) and crash logs. This does not include your name or contact details and is used only to understand how the game is played and to fix bugs. In the EEA, the UK and similar regions, if you decline consent, advertising-related data is not stored. Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID, device information and approximate location (from IP address) to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and similar regions you will be asked for consent on first launch, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy Options (Ads). How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

"Remove Ads" is paid through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see your card or payment information.

Backup

Android Auto Backup may save your game progress to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device. This is handled by Google and can be turned off in your device settings.

Children

This game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all game data from your device.
